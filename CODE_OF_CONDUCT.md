# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the BanglaScript community a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Welcoming questions, differing viewpoints, and accessible explanations.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and any other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through the private contact
method described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please do not use a public
issue for sensitive reports. All reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies wherever an individual represents the BanglaScript
project or participates in its community spaces. It also applies when conduct
has a significant impact on the safety or experience of the project community.
